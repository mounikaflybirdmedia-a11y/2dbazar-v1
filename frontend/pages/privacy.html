Privacy Policy

How we handle and protect your data

1. Information We Collect

At 2DBazaar, we are committed to protecting your privacy. We collect information you provide directly to us when you register, post a listing, or contact support. This includes your name, email address, phone number, postal address, profile picture, payment details, and any other data you choose to share. We also automatically collect certain technical data when you use our platform, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and browsing behavior to help us optimize your experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

The data we collect is used strictly to provide, maintain, and enhance our services. Specifically, we use your information to: facilitate secure connections between buyers, sellers, and service providers; process transactions and send payment receipts; personalize your user dashboard; enforce our community guidelines; send administrative updates and promotional offers (which you can opt out of at any time); and perform internal analytics to improve the platform's performance.

3. Sharing of Information

To make 2DBazaar work, we must share limited information between users. For instance, if you book a plumbing service or apply for a job, your contact details and location will be shared with the relevant provider or employer. We may also share data with trusted third-party vendors (such as payment processors and cloud hosting providers) who assist us in operating the platform. We will never sell your personal data to advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Retention and Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including encryption and secure server hosting, to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. We retain your data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law. You have the right to request the deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

2DBazaar utilizes cookies and similar tracking technologies to remember your preferences, keep you logged in, and understand how you interact with our website. You can configure your browser to refuse all cookies, but please note that some features of our platform may not function properly without them.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding your data and this Privacy Policy, please contact our Data Protection Officer at 2dbazaar.com@gmail.com or reach out to our support team directly via WhatsApp.
