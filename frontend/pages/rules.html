Rules & Regulations

Community guidelines for a safe environment

1. Authentic and Accurate Listings

Transparency is the foundation of 2DBazaar. All services, job postings, marketplace products, and real estate properties must be 100% authentic and accurately described. You must not use misleading titles, exaggerated claims, fake images, or incorrect pricing. Deliberately deceptive listings will be permanently removed, and repeat offenders will face account bans.

2. Safe and Respectful Communication

We mandate a zero-tolerance policy for harassment, hate speech, discrimination, threats, or abusive language. Whether you are interacting through WhatsApp, phone calls, or platform messages, you are expected to maintain a professional and courteous demeanor towards service providers, sellers, buyers, and 2DBazaar support staff.

3. Prohibited and Restricted Items

The listing of illegal items, counterfeit goods, hazardous materials, weapons, adult content, and unregulated medical products is strictly forbidden. Furthermore, multi-level marketing (MLM) schemes, pyramid schemes, and spam/scam job postings are prohibited. Our moderation team actively monitors the platform to ensure compliance with this rule.

4. Transaction Safety and Dispute Resolution

For your safety, we strongly advise meeting in public places for physical item exchanges and thoroughly verifying service providers before granting them access to your home or office. While 2DBazaar facilitates connections, any disputes regarding payment, service quality, or item condition must be resolved directly between the involved parties. We encourage leaving honest, objective reviews to help the community.

5. Duplicate Listings and Spam

To maintain a clean and easily navigable marketplace, users are prohibited from posting duplicate listings for the same product, job, or service across multiple categories or locations. Spamming the platform degrades the user experience and will result in listing deletion and potential account restrictions.

6. Compliance with Indian Laws

All activities conducted on 2DBazaar must fully comply with the local, state, and federal laws of India. Users engaged in fraudulent schemes, money laundering, or any other criminal activities will be reported to the appropriate law enforcement authorities.
